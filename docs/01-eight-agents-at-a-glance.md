# 1) The Eight Agents at a Glance

| # | Agent | Executive | Main scenario | Key knowledge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CEO Daily Brief | CEO | Daily brief from email, Teams, meetings: decisions, escalations, prep, follow-ups | Email, Teams chats/meetings, People, ExCo SharePoint |
| 2 | Board Pack Navigator | Chair and Board | Navigate board pack: summaries, decisions sought, risks, actions, director questions | Board papers library, minutes, board meetings |
| 3 | CFO Performance Analyst | CFO | Variance analysis, what-if modeling, charts, finance commentary | Management accounts, budget/forecast, finance sites |
| 4 | Operations Control Tower | COO | Single operational view: RAG, slippage, overdue mitigations, escalations | Programme/RAID list, PMO site, operations chats/meetings |
| 5 | People and Culture Advisor | CHRO | Policy Q&A with citations, aggregated people insights, labour checks, HR communications | HR policies, aggregated workforce/surveys, regulator site |
| 6 | Tech and AI Portfolio Advisor | CIO/CDO | Prioritise tech+AI investments, governance checks, adoption insights | AI use-case list, governance policy, adoption exports |
| 7 | Market Intelligence Advisor | CMO/CCO | Market briefs, battlecards, VOC themes, campaign messaging | Web/competitor sites, market research, customer meetings, CRM |
| 8 | Regulatory and Risk Radar | CRO/GC | Track regulation, assess policy impact, summarize enterprise risk | Regulator sites, policy library, risk register list |
